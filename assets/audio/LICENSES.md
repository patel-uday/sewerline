# Audio licenses and sources

All downloaded audio in this directory is released under the Creative Commons Zero (CC0) public-domain dedication. Attribution is not required, but the provenance is recorded here for clarity.

## Free Firearm Sound Library / BigSoundBank

The following files were obtained through the `hard-lines` project's audio mirror and source manifest:

- `pistol.wav` — `p226-report-0.wav`, Free Firearm Sound Library, CC0.
- `footstep_0.wav` — `step-hard-0.wav`, BigSoundBank, CC0.
- `footstep_1.wav` — `step-hard-1.wav`, BigSoundBank, CC0.

Source manifest: https://github.com/yegors/hard-lines/blob/main/public/audio/README.md

## Kenney game audio

The following files were obtained through the `jinglescript` project's audio samples and source manifest. The originals are from Kenney's CC0 game-audio collections:

- `melee.wav` — `punch-hit.wav`, Kenney Impact Sounds, CC0.
- `drop.wav` — `book-place.wav`, Kenney RPG Audio, CC0.
- `pickup.wav` — `bell-ding.wav`, Kenney Impact Sounds, CC0.

Source manifest: https://github.com/receptron/jinglescript/blob/main/samples/sfx/SOURCES.md

Kenney license policy: https://kenney.nl/support
